### 6월 9일 (일) 13:03 · Grand Canyon RIM2RIM (via Bright Angel T…

Grand Canyon RIM2RIM (via Bright Angel Trail, 27km with 1500m elevation gain): one item in my bucket list DONE! It was the most challenging trail running in my life so far. It's a very unique experience in that I have to down first and climbing up later.

TLDR: Rim to the river was fun, and river to Indian Garden is manageable; Indian Garden to Rim is hell. However, the trail is absolutely beautiful.

Rim to Indian Garden: I started at 4:43 AM to beat the heat and sun. The trail was absolutely beautiful, and my steps are light and almost feel like flying.

Indian Garden to River: After 8km trailing running, my steps are getting heavier, but it was still downhill. The view is also fantastic and somewhat different from the first part.

River: I finally met the Colorado River. Looking up Grand Canyon from the river is totally overwhelming. I stayed there for 20 min. Now, the downhill part is done!

River to Indian Garden: Actually, downhill was just warming up, and now the hiking starts. Going up is much harder with already tired legs. However, the trail was under the shadow, and I managed it well.

Indian Garden to Three Mile Resthouse: It's just a 3km, but the sun came out. I have to push each step. Getting hot and worried.

Three Mile Resthouse to Rim: I am totally exhausted, but still 5KM more to go. The trail and environment were merciless: continues uphill and extream heat, no shade. Each step with tired legs is extremely hard. But each step counts!

At the Rim (again): with five hours running/hiking + one hour break, average heart rate 155bpm. I am done, and it was totally worth it.
